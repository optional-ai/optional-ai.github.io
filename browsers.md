# Chrome
## Disable On-device AI
Settings > System > Toggle "On-device AI"
- Described as powering features such as "scam detection": https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/16961953?visit_id=639198805039182414-2961728128&p=on_device_genA 

# Edge
## Disable Copilot
Settings > Copilot and AI > Toggle "Show Copilot button in toolbar"
- This will also ensure disabling of Copilot to use browser content
