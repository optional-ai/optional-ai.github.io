# Turn off Copilot
The following applies whether using Teams in the browser or desktop app:
- Settings > Chat and Channels > Toggle "Copilot" off

_Note_: while this removes Copilot from the list under the _Chats_ tab, it doesn't remove the standalone _Copilot_ tab, rendering it fairly redundant.

General guidance across Microsoft 365 apps can be found here: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/turn-off-copilot-in-microsoft-365-apps
